    Is delivery free?

    Orders of ₹1,000 or more, after the 10% discount, ship free anywhere in India. Below that, the courier charge is worked out from your pincode and the parcel weight and shown in your cart.

    How do I order?

    Add jars to your cart, log in with your mobile number, pick a delivery address and send the order to us on WhatsApp. We confirm it in the chat.

    Where does your honey come from?

    From carefully chosen regions of India: Multiflora from Himachal Pradesh and Punjab, Kashmiri Acacia from Kashmir and Mustard from Hanumangarh in Rajasthan. Each variety reflects the flowers of its origin.

    Is the honey raw?

    Yes. Every Ahluwalia Farm honey is raw & unprocessed, bottled in square glass jars of 250g, 500g and 1kg.

    Why is the Mustard honey so thick?

    Mustard honey sets naturally and quickly into a creamy, spoonable texture. That's how it should be: it's the same honey, just in its natural form.

    Are your eggs cage-free?

    Yes. They come from BV 380 laying hens raised cage-free across eight partner farms around Amritsar, run by farmers trained through Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).

    Do you supply shops and distributors?

    We do. Message the team on WhatsApp or write to us, and we'll talk through the product range, supply schedule and partnership terms for your business.
